# Every option: Forever Companion 1.9.5 + FHK Gear 0.5.3

Generated from the addons' real options pages: this is exactly what you see in EllesmereUI's options, in the **Forever Companion** and **Gear** sidebar entries.

- **Everything starts off.** Turn on what you want.
- _(Class names)_ after a section or option mean only those classes see it. Options follow the character you log in with.
- **settings** are in the option's cog, **position** in its move arrows, **colors** in its swatches, and **preview** is the eye that shows it for a few seconds.
- Most bars and icons also move in Ellesmere's **Unlock Mode**, and every section has a **Reset**.
- Your class page (the first Forever Companion page) repeats your class's main rows, with links to their full sections.

What changed in this release: [WHATS_NEW.md](WHATS_NEW.md). Install: [QUICKSTART.md](QUICKSTART.md).

## Forever Companion

In EllesmereUI's options sidebar: **Forever Companion**. Its first page is named after your class and gathers that class's features (with links to each); the pages below hold every option. Everything starts off.

### General

**Forever Theme Presets**

- **Dark Mode**: Applies the Dark preset to health and resource bars across the suite.
- **Restore Original Look**
- **Forever Theme Preset**: Colored, Dark and Afterglow use native color sharing.
- **Strong Theme Outlines**: Keeps native fonts and sizes, with stronger black outlines on world cues and finer outlines on small bar labels.
- **Readable Colored Fills**: Your health starts in your class color, then warns gold, orange and red.
- **Pixel Icon Edges**: Adds black pixel borders to square cue icons and a thin black edge around transparent status symbols.
- **Health / Power Separator**: A black physical-pixel line between health and power on custom unit frames.

**Forever Motion**

- **Reduce Companion Motion**: Warnings stay steady instead of pulsing, history icons fade without sliding, cue fades and XP fill are instant, and the XP glow and damage trail are off.
- **Show Advanced Companion Options**: Shows fine-tuning rows (sizes, offsets, opacities, custom colors) in every Forever section.
- **Preview Scenario**: Shows the same fixed state every time for about ten seconds, to compare styles. (preview)

**Forever Chat**

- **Hide Chat In Combat**: Uses Ellesmere's own visibility rules: chat hides as combat starts and returns when it ends.
- **Show Chat Only When Typing Or Hovered**: Chat stays hidden and appears while you type or hover it, fading 2 seconds after the mouse leaves.

### Action Bars

**Key Labels and Menu**

- **Keyboard-First Key Labels**: A button bound to a keyboard key and a Naga button shows the keyboard key (SG, not SF12); Insert and Delete shorten to Ins and Del.
- **Menu And Bags Visibility**: Show On Hover hides the menu and bag bar, including the keyring, until hovered in or out of combat.

**XP Bar Refinements**

- **Completed Quest XP**: Shows known XP from completed quests awaiting turn-in. (settings: Opacity %; colors: Completed Quest XP Color)
- **Smooth XP Fill**: Eases gains and snaps when the level changes.
- **XP Gain Glow**: Briefly highlights the experience you just earned. (settings: Opacity %)
- **10% XP Ticks**: Replaces native dividers with subtle ten-percent marks. (settings: Opacity %)
- **Session XP Tooltip**: Adds session XP, XP per hour and estimated time to level.
- **XP Number Format**: How XP numbers show.
- **Default Quest XP Color**: Returns the completed quest XP to the theme color.
- **Reset XP Bar Refinements**: Restores the XP Bar Refinements settings to their defaults.

**Key Press and Action History**

- **Flash Every Action Press**: A short accent border responds to each key-down, even when the ability cannot fire. (settings: Opacity %, Duration; colors: Press Flash Color)
- **Recent Action History**: A separate movable unit in native Unlock Mode. (settings: Icon Count, Icon Size, Icon Spacing, Icon Zoom, Direction, Hide After Idle (sec), Opacity %, Older Icon Opacity %; preview)
- **Open Native Spell History**
- **History Records**: Key Presses: every attempted press.
- **Fold Repeated Presses**: Groups consecutive presses of the same binding with an xN count.
- **Show History Key Labels**: Bright label: the key was seen held. (settings: Font Size, Position)
- **Include Mouse Clicks**: Includes mouse clicks in press history; labels them Click or RClick.
- **Naga Button Labels**: Shows N1-N6 for F9-F12 / Insert / Delete, N9 / N11 / N12 for the Synapse chords, instead of the keys they send.
- **Move / Resize History**: Opens Unlock Mode, where Recent Actions moves and resizes like Ellesmere elements.
- **Reset Key Press And Action History**: Restores the press flash and action history settings to their defaults, which are off.

## FHK Gear

In EllesmereUI's options sidebar: **Gear**.

### Automation

**Automatic Actions**

- **Auto-Equip Upgrades**: Equips better gear from your bags out of combat.
- **Auto-Pick Quest Rewards**: Takes the best upgrade, or the highest vendor value when nothing is an upgrade.
- **Auto-Roll on Loot**: Need on upgrades you may Need on, Greed on other gear.
- **Levelling Mode**: Automatic actions run only at levels 1-59.
- **Auto-Equip Up To**: Automatic equipping includes this rarity and below.
- **Rarity Cap: Bind on Equip Only**: On: the rarity cap holds back only items you could still sell or trade (Bind on Equip or unbound).
- **Auto-Equip Empty Bag Upgrades**: Allows empty bag and suitable quiver upgrades.
- **Auto-Equip Better Ammo**: Puts the best usable arrows or bullets for your ranged weapon in the ammo slot, including a higher tier the moment you reach its level.

**Pop-Ups**

- **Action Pop-Ups**: A card when Gear equips an item, picks a quest reward or rolls on loot.
- **Upgrade Found Pop-Ups**: A card with Equip and Never Equip when an upgrade lands in your bags that Gear leaves to you.
- **Pop-Up Duration**: Seconds a card stays; hovering keeps it.
- **Pop-Up Preview**: Shows two sample cards.

**Bind on Equip**

- **Auto-Equip Bind-on-Equip**: Lets auto-equip use bind-on-equip items up to Bind on Equip Up To (and your Auto-Equip rarity).
- **Auto-Confirm Bind Prompt**: Accepts the bind prompt, but only for the item Gear is equipping.
- **Bind on Equip Up To**: Bind-on-equip upgrades are equipped only at this rarity and below; rarer ones stay in your bags, marked, to sell or trade.

**Loot Rolls**

- **Need on Upgrades**: Auto-Roll chooses Need only for supported upgrades.
- **Greed on Other Loot**: Auto-Roll chooses Greed only when the roll permits it.
- **Minimum Need Gain**: Need only when the upgrade adds at least this percent to the score of the item it replaces.
- **Non-Gear Loot Rolls**: Loot that is not gear (cloth, reagents, food).
- **Non-Gear Up To**: Non-gear loot of this rarity and below is rolled for you; rarer loot is your roll.
- **Need Only My Armor Type**: Need only on cloth, leather, mail or plate you are meant to wear (yours now: Leather).
- **Need Only My Main Stat**: Need only on gear that has your main stat (now Agility), or no Strength, Agility or Intellect at all.
- **Auto-Confirm Roll Prompt**: Accepts the bind prompt only for a Need or Greed roll Gear made.
- **Chat Messages**: A chat line for each automatic action that shows no pop-up card.
