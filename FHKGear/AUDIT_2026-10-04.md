## 2. Summary

FHK Gear starts with a passing mock suite and improves materially on AutoGear's permanent polling: it has no OnUpdate loop, a bounded item cache, weak score-cache keys and an equipped snapshot for repeated tooltip comparisons. Its automation is off by default. It is still an approximate scoring engine with reproducible unsafe edges. The highest priorities are ownership of bind confirmations, incomplete item data, irreversible quest baselines and deferred action lifetimes. It cannot currently establish a DPS increase for arbitrary levelling abilities or complete weapon setups.

## 3. Top 10 fixes

1. **G01** - Bind confirmation ownership: an unrelated manual item can be bound inside Gear's time window.
2. **G02** - Complete item data before verdicts: an unusable reward can be cached as usable.
3. **G03** - Quest baseline must include available gear: otherwise Gear can consume a reward on a redundant bow.
4. **G04** - Cancel deferred quest work: a closed or replaced dialog can receive an old reward action.
5. **G12** - Confirm actual equip success and try fallbacks: current failure handling can stall without equipping anything.
6. **G05** - Compare full MH/OH setups: a better pair cannot replace a worn two-hander.
7. **G07** - Distinguish duplicate copies: an ordinary second ring/trinket/weapon can be rejected.
8. **G08** - Enforce unique-equipped categories: a flagged replacement can form an illegal setup.
9. **G09** - Exclude inactive set effects: they currently create a false stat advantage.
10. **G11** - Reconcile equivalent stat sources: alias/Equip duplication can change the chosen item.

G06, G43 and G59 are also High and belong in the same first repair round. Scoring-model expansion should follow the action/data safeguards, because a better damage formula cannot make an unsafe action transaction reliable.

## 4. Findings

Paths in findings are relative to Interface/AddOns/FHKGear unless otherwise stated. API evidence and uncertainty are qualified above. Effort: S = small local change; M = several related paths/fixtures; L = new model or integration.

### G01. Bind confirmation can accept another item

- **Severity:** Critical
- **Dimension:** 1. Correctness
- **Where:** Core.lua:185; Core.lua:209
- **Evidence:** E2 (mock/script reproduction; no in-game verification)
- **Problem:** bindWindow remembers a link but never checks it or the target slot. Any EQUIP_BIND_CONFIRM within three seconds is accepted, even after a successful non-binding equip.
- **Failure scenario:** Gear equips a grey bow into slot 18. A manual chest equip opens a bind prompt for slot 5 immediately afterwards. The mock confirms slot 5.
- **Recommendation:** Record an equip transaction with source location, item identity and target. Confirm only a matching pending equip. Clear the transaction on success, failure, cancellation and disable; do not use elapsed time as proof of ownership.
- **Effort:** M
- **Status vs HANDOVER:** new

### G02. Incomplete tooltip data is cached as usable

- **Severity:** Critical
- **Dimension:** 4. Robustness and safety
- **Where:** Items.lua:143; Items.lua:198; Items.lua:210; Items.lua:247
- **Evidence:** E2 (mock/script reproduction; no in-game verification)
- **Problem:** A loaded name is enough to cache the item. Empty tooltip lines or unavailable structured stats do not make the record pending. Requirements and effects can be absent permanently until invalidation.
- **Failure scenario:** GetItemInfo returns the untrained gun's name and DPS; GetHyperlink returns lines={}. Read caches usable=true, and Verdict gives +40 into the empty ranged slot. A later complete tooltip still returns that cached record. Quest automation could choose this unusable reward.
- **Recommendation:** Require complete public item/tooltip data before issuing a verdict. Track requirements and stats readiness separately. Subscribe to the relevant item-data/tooltip update while incomplete, and invalidate partial records when data arrives.
- **Effort:** M
- **Status vs HANDOVER:** new

### G03. Quest choice ignores better gear already in bags

- **Severity:** Critical
- **Dimension:** 1. Correctness
- **Where:** Engine.lua:240; Engine.lua:243; Engine.lua:252
- **Evidence:** E2 (mock/script reproduction; no in-game verification)
- **Problem:** QuestChoice compares rewards only with equipped gear. The installed AutoGear comparison includes usable bag gear, so this loses a useful safeguard for an irreversible choice.
- **Failure scenario:** Wear a 5.77 DPS bow and the 3 Agility vest. Bags contain a usable 20 DPS bow. Rewards are a 7 DPS bow and a 4 Agility chest. QuestChoice chooses the bow, which adds nothing to the available setup; the chest improves it.
- **Recommendation:** Build one baseline from the best legal worn/bag setup, honouring locks and exclusions. Compare each reward independently against that baseline. Do not let one mutually exclusive reward become another reward's baseline.
- **Effort:** M
- **Status vs HANDOVER:** new

### G04. Quest callbacks survive the quest window

- **Severity:** High
- **Dimension:** 1. Correctness
- **Where:** Core.lua:225; Core.lua:240
- **Evidence:** E2 (mock/script reproduction; no in-game verification)
- **Problem:** QUEST_FINISHED hides borders but does not cancel QuestPass or its item-data callbacks. Work is not tied to a quest ID or dialog generation.
- **Failure scenario:** Fire QUEST_COMPLETE with autoQuest on, then QUEST_FINISHED before the timer. With reward data still returned, the mock calls GetQuestReward(1) after closure. A newly opened quest can receive an old callback too.
- **Recommendation:** Use a quest generation/ID and reward-stage guard in every deferred pass. Cancel outstanding work on QUEST_FINISHED and disable. Recheck the generation before GetQuestReward.
- **Effort:** M
- **Status vs HANDOVER:** new

### G05. A better MH/OH pair cannot replace a two-hander

- **Severity:** High
- **Dimension:** 1. Correctness
- **Where:** Engine.lua:189; Engine.lua:196; Engine.lua:214
- **Evidence:** E2 (mock/script reproduction; no in-game verification)
- **Problem:** Verdict evaluates individual candidates, not complete setups. Off hands are blocked while a two-hander is worn, and a weaker standalone main hand cannot start a better pair.
- **Failure scenario:** Wear a two-hander with 12 Agility. Bags contain a 7 Agility one-hander and a 7 Agility off-hand weapon; dual wield is available. BagUpgrades returns zero jobs although the legal pair totals 14.
- **Recommendation:** Evaluate legal ordered main/off-hand pairs and two-handers together, including bag partners. Queue an entire winning transaction in a legal order with room for displaced gear. Keep global stats separate from each hand's damage contribution.
- **Effort:** L
- **Status vs HANDOVER:** known gap (confirmed)

### G06. Weapon-style constraints are only partly enforced

- **Severity:** High
- **Dimension:** 1. Correctness
- **Where:** Engine.lua:28; Data/Weights.lua:244; Data/Weights.lua:396
- **Evidence:** E2 (mock/script reproduction; no in-game verification)
- **Problem:** INVTYPE_WEAPONMAINHAND bypasses the '2h' check. 'dagger and any' is never enforced by subclass. This discards constraints present in AutoGear.
- **Failure scenario:** Slots accepts a main-hand-only sword for Arms with weapons='2h', and for an Assassination Rogue with weapons='dagger and any'. Replacing a Rogue's dagger can remove access to learned Backstab.
- **Recommendation:** Apply style and subclass rules consistently to every weapon inventory type and ordered pair. Derive ability requirements from learned spells; keep player weapon preferences separate from client equip legality. Verify Backstab's requirement in game; its client-data reference requires a main-hand dagger: [ForeverDB Backstab](https://foreverdb.net/spell/53).
- **Effort:** M
- **Status vs HANDOVER:** known gap (confirmed)

### G07. A second ordinary copy is mistaken for the worn item

- **Severity:** High
- **Dimension:** 1. Correctness
- **Where:** Engine.lua:186; Markers.lua:15
- **Evidence:** E2 (mock/script reproduction; no in-game verification)
- **Problem:** A matching item link causes an immediate return before the other valid slot is examined. Tooltip marking also equates matching links with the same physical item.
- **Failure scenario:** Wear non-unique item:8 in ring slot 11, leave slot 12 empty, and put a second identical ring in the bag. Verdict returns nil and the bag copy is labelled equipped. Identical non-unique trinkets and one-handers have the same problem.
- **Recommendation:** Use location/GUID when identifying the actual worn object. For link-only comparisons, continue to other slots and enforce uniqueness separately. A normal duplicate may fill the second slot; the same physical bag item must never be used twice in a setup.
- **Effort:** M
- **Status vs HANDOVER:** new

### G08. Unique-equipped categories are not enforced

- **Severity:** High
- **Dimension:** 1. Correctness
- **Where:** Items.lua:228; Engine.lua:198
- **Evidence:** E2 (mock/script reproduction; no in-game verification)
- **Problem:** Only exact Unique/Unique-Equipped text and the same item ID are handled. Category names and limits are not stored or checked.
- **Failure scenario:** Two different trinkets both say 'Unique-Equipped: Test Category (1)'. With the first worn, the second receives a +2.1 verdict into the other slot. The mock shows unique=nil.
- **Recommendation:** Read category/limit from a verified public item API where available, with a tested tooltip fallback. Validate the entire proposed setup, including items in other slot types. Confirm API runtime availability before depending on it.
- **Effort:** M
- **Status vs HANDOVER:** known gap (confirmed)

### G09. Inactive set procs are scored as active

- **Severity:** High
- **Dimension:** 2. Scoring model
- **Where:** Items.lua:230; Items.lua:231; Items.lua:105
- **Evidence:** E2 (mock/script reproduction; no in-game verification)
- **Problem:** The exclusion matches only a line beginning 'set:'. Ordinary '(2) Set:' prefixes pass through AddProc, without checking set count or grey inactive text.
- **Failure scenario:** A lone item has '(2) Set: Chance on hit: Increases your agility by 60 for 10 sec.' Read creates one proc and scores +10.5 despite the two-piece threshold being unmet.
- **Recommendation:** Recognise set lines before standalone effects. Store set identity, threshold and effect; apply them only when the complete candidate setup meets that threshold. Until supported, keep every set line explicitly unscored.
- **Effort:** M
- **Status vs HANDOVER:** known gap (wrong/outdated)

### G10. Rating fields are treated as percentage points

- **Severity:** Medium
- **Dimension:** 2. Scoring model
- **Where:** Weights.lua:31; Weights.lua:77; Weights.lua:96
- **Evidence:** E1 (source inspection)
- **Problem:** Structured *_RATING_* values map directly to per-1% stats. Probe §7.1 confirms the stat table exists, but not the units of a crit/hit item. This is an unresolved conversion risk, not a confirmed server conversion.
- **Failure scenario:** If a structured CritRating value of 14 represents 1%, Hunter scoring awards 294 rather than 21. The current model makes no distinction. Explicit tooltip 'by 1%' and raw rating fields enter the same stat.
- **Recommendation:** Separate rating and percent facts. Record source/unit and apply only a client/build/level conversion verified for Forever. Capture a real crit/hit item's raw table, tooltip and character-sheet change first; do not infer units from the key alone. [ForeverDB's sim documentation](https://foreverdb.net/sim) reports separate rating conversions but is not an in-game confirmation.
- **Effort:** M
- **Status vs HANDOVER:** known gap (confirmed)

### G11. Equivalent stat sources can double count

- **Severity:** High
- **Dimension:** 2. Scoring model
- **Where:** Weights.lua:31; Weights.lua:35; Items.lua:215; Items.lua:238
- **Evidence:** E2 (mock/script reproduction; no in-game verification)
- **Problem:** De-duplication operates only on the final exact stat name. Alias keys are summed; an equivalent damage-only Equip line can be counted in addition to structured spell damage mapped to SpellPower.
- **Failure scenario:** The synthetic raw table {generic crit=1, melee crit=1, ranged crit=1} yields Crit=3. A damage-only item with ITEM_MOD_SPELL_DAMAGE_DONE_SHORT=10 plus its matching magical damage Equip line yields SpellPower=10 and SpellDamage=10.
- **Recommendation:** Normalise equivalent keys with defined precedence, preserving truly different melee/ranged/spell stats. Reconcile structured facts and lines by effect and unit. Keep fixtures for redundant aliases and genuinely separate bonuses; capture actual Forever payloads to settle which aliases coexist.
- **Effort:** M
- **Status vs HANDOVER:** new

### G12. Failed equips are not confirmed and can hide fallbacks

- **Severity:** High
- **Dimension:** 1. Correctness
- **Where:** Core.lua:180; Core.lua:186; Core.lua:194; Engine.lua:225
- **Evidence:** E2 (mock/script reproduction; no in-game verification)
- **Problem:** An empty cursor is treated as success, with no inventory confirmation. Failures are filtered only after BagUpgrades has kept the single best item for a target.
- **Failure scenario:** A no-op pickup prints 'Equipped item:29' while item:1 is still worn. In a separate fixture, item:29 fails three times and remains in the bag; the eligible item:2 is never attempted because item:29 keeps winning bySlot.
- **Recommendation:** Track pickup, pending confirmation and inventory acknowledgement separately. Recheck the source identity before pickup. Exclude temporarily failed candidates before selecting the best per slot, retry ready fallbacks, and expire failure counts when relevant state changes. Preserve cursor contents safely on API errors.
- **Effort:** M
- **Status vs HANDOVER:** new

### G13. A locked best bag item blocks unrelated upgrades

- **Severity:** Medium
- **Dimension:** 1. Correctness
- **Where:** Core.lua:183
- **Evidence:** E1 (source inspection)
- **Problem:** The first locked or unavailable bag slot returns from the whole pass and schedules another scan. Ready jobs for other targets are not tried.
- **Failure scenario:** The best cloak is temporarily locked by a trade or bag operation. A ready bow upgrade follows it in jobs. Every retry revisits the cloak first, leaving the bow untouched for the entire lock.
- **Recommendation:** Skip locked candidates for this pass and continue with other targets. Keep one bounded deferred retry or wake from bag-unlock changes; do not let a permanently unavailable top job monopolise the queue.
- **Effort:** S
- **Status vs HANDOVER:** new

### G14. Loot rolling does not honour Greed availability

- **Severity:** Medium
- **Dimension:** 1. Correctness
- **Where:** Engine.lua:271; Engine.lua:274; Core.lua:247
- **Evidence:** E2 (mock/script reproduction; no in-game verification)
- **Problem:** Only canNeed is read. The fallback is always Greed, and no roll-expiry/cancellation guard protects deferred retries.
- **Failure scenario:** GetLootRollItemInfo returns canNeed=false and canGreed=false; RollChoice still returns 2. A pending callback can also act after the roll has ended.
- **Recommendation:** Read all relevant eligibility flags, choose an allowed policy action or leave it manual, and verify the roll remains active. Cancel retries and clear myRolls on cancellation/completion; bound that table's lifetime.
- **Effort:** M
- **Status vs HANDOVER:** new

### G15. Named weight import drops digit keys and misreads exponents

- **Severity:** Medium
- **Dimension:** 2. Scoring model
- **Where:** Weights.lua:205; Weights.lua:208; Options.lua:135
- **Evidence:** E2 (mock/script reproduction; no in-game verification)
- **Problem:** The key pattern accepts letters only, so Mp5 and Hp5 cannot import despite appearing in the map/UI. Scientific notation is silently truncated; repeated aliases are added.
- **Failure scenario:** Parse 'Agility=1, Mp5=4, Hp5=2, Ap=1e-3': Mp5/Hp5 are nil and AttackPower becomes 1 rather than 0.001. 'Agility=1, Agility=1' also passes the two-stat rule.
- **Recommendation:** Tokenise full key=value entries, including digits and valid numeric notation, and validate the whole value. Reject malformed/duplicate entries with a visible error. Count distinct usable stats.
- **Effort:** S
- **Status vs HANDOVER:** new

### G16. Pawn export loses effective weights and units

- **Severity:** Medium
- **Dimension:** 2. Scoring model
- **Where:** Weights.lua:13; Weights.lua:222; Weights.lua:225; Options.lua:145
- **Evidence:** E2 (mock/script reproduction; no in-game verification)
- **Problem:** Export omits explicit zeroes, generic DPS and the separate SpellDamage value. Per-percent Crit/Hit are exported under rating names without a conversion contract. Rounded output is not a faithful backup.
- **Failure scenario:** Export Mage Fire weights with SpellCrit=0 and SpellDamage=7. Reimport loses the zero, reconstructs damage as 1.1 from SpellPower, and drops generic weapon DPS. Defaults can reintroduce the deliberately zeroed stat.
- **Recommendation:** Define a lossless FHK weight format for backup and separate it from compatible Pawn sharing. Preserve explicit zeroes and independent damage/healing fields. Either convert verified units for Pawn or clearly identify incompatible scales; include generic weapon DPS or both split fields.
- **Effort:** M
- **Status vs HANDOVER:** new

### Default weights: all nine classes and specs

This review applies to Data/Weights.lua and Weights.Defaults. "None" matters because it is the real detected fallback in the available probe. Built-in Levelling and Endgame phases use the same defaults; only the player's separate custom tables make their weights differ.

No class/spec has a validated marginal-DPS calibration in this workspace. The role rewrite sets physical Crit/Hit to 20/18 times the largest selected offensive stat weight, caster SpellCrit/SpellHit to 11/10 times SpellPower (minimum 0.5), and healer SpellCrit to 7 times that value. Tank values use another fixed set. These are heuristics. Rewriting inherited numbers does not validate their relative priorities.

#### What an ability-aware levelling model would need

Evaluate the complete legal worn/bag setup, then estimate each learned ability's damage from its rank, slot requirements, weapon contribution, AP/SP coefficient and verified talent changes. Multiply by feasible casts/swings during an explicit fight. Account for cooldowns, resource limits, auto attacks, short-lived DoTs, pet damage and recovery. Compare the entire setup's result; derive optional local stat weights by small changes around that setup.

For Hunter, start with auto shot, learned Arcane Shot/Aimed Shot/Multi-Shot, melee participation and pet contribution. Store weapon speed and damage range. Verify shot timing and normalization before claiming that a slower bow always wins. [ForeverDB Aimed Shot](https://foreverdb.net/spell/19434) identifies a weapon-based learned ability; that alone does not establish the optimum bow speed.

Do not ship invented rotations for nine classes. Add measured class/rank scenarios incrementally, show approximate/unknown state, and leave close unknown-effect decisions manual. Survival, healer and solo-damage objectives need explicit labels. School coefficients alone do not determine their weights.

#### External simulator distinction

MythicSim character JSON is not a Pawn scale. Its [official page](https://mythicsim.com/wow-forever/sim) describes a gear/rotation simulator with Best Gear search and an exporter; its default raid context is not a levelling objective. Gear's delegated exporter button is the right kind of bridge, but importing its output as stat weights would be a separate, specified integration. No objective ranking of "top sim site" was established here.

ForeverGear stays a separate installed public-API score source. API readiness and a supported numeric result must be checked independently. The audit used its public contract and synthetic provider fixtures; it did not inspect its scoring implementation.
